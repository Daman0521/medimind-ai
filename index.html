Patient Safety & Guidance

Health Information & Clinical Boundaries

Understanding how MediMind AI works, what it can responsibly assist with, and when human healthcare professionals are required.

What MediMind AI Can Do

  • Symptom Exploration: Discuss symptoms to understand potential categories of causes to discuss with a doctor.
  • Medical Terminology: Explain clinical terms, lab tests, and procedures in clear, everyday language.
  • Doctor Visit Preparation: Help formulate thoughtful questions and organize your symptom timeline before an appointment.
  • General Wellness: Provide evidence-aligned lifestyle, nutrition, hydration, and sleep hygiene information.
  • Follow-Up Clarifications: Answer subsequent questions to help you better understand health concepts.

What MediMind AI Cannot Do

  • No Clinical Diagnosis: Cannot provide a definitive diagnosis or replace physical examinations.
  • No Medication Prescriptions: Cannot prescribe medications, adjust dosages, or recommend specific pharmaceuticals.
  • No Altering Prescribed Care: Will never recommend stopping or changing doctor-prescribed medications or treatments.
  • No Emergency Triage: Cannot manage life-threatening emergencies or critical trauma cases.
  • No Absolute Guarantees: AI models can make errors, hallucinate, or misinterpret nuances.

Recognizing Medical Emergencies

Seek emergency medical care immediately if you or someone else experiences any of the following critical warning signs:

  • Sudden Chest Pain or Pressure: Squeezing sensation radiating to arm, jaw, neck, or back.
  • Signs of Stroke (F.A.S.T.): Face drooping, Arm weakness, Slurred speech, Time to call emergency.
  • Severe Shortness of Breath: Gasping, inability to speak full sentences, or blue lips.
  • Loss of Consciousness or Confusion: Sudden fainting, unresponsiveness, or abrupt cognitive changes.
  • Uncontrolled Bleeding or Severe Head Trauma: Significant injuries or profuse blood loss.
Emergency Helplines:
India: 112 / 102 USA: 911 UK: 999 EU: 112

Image & Video Capabilities

MediMind AI prepares for multimodal medical discussions through n8n workflows and Google Gemini:

  • Images: Once backend processing is active, image uploads (e.g. skin photos, lab values) serve as visual context to enrich the conversation.
  • Video Processing: Video analysis requires specialized backend frame extraction. If not yet configured, the system explicitly informs you so expectations remain accurate.
  • Visual Limitations: Skin lighting, camera resolution, angle, and compression can distort medical appearances. AI cannot replace in-person dermatological or clinical review.

Privacy Best Practices

  • Do Not Share Personal Identifiers: Never provide your full legal name, home address, social security number, Aadhaar number, or phone numbers.
  • Scrub Medical Documents: Crop or redact patient names and hospital identification numbers before sharing report snippets.
  • Local Storage Control: Conversation titles and text messages are cached locally in your browser so you can revisit them. You can clear them at any time with one click.
  • No Image Persistence: Medical media files are not stored persistently in your browser's local storage.

About MediMind AI

Your AI Health Information Companion

Bridging the gap between confusing medical jargon and patient understanding through transparent, responsible artificial intelligence.

Our Mission

MediMind AI was created to provide accessible, empathetic, and clear general health guidance to everyday individuals. When people feel unwell or receive complex lab results, navigating medical terms online often leads to confusion or undue anxiety.

Our goal is to assist users in structuring their health questions, understanding potential explanations, and learning what warning signs warrant timely consultation with a qualified medical professional.

Technology Stack

System Architecture

  • Modern Vanilla Frontend: Fast, responsive, zero-dependency HTML5, CSS3, and modern JavaScript running cleanly in any browser.
  • n8n Automation Engine: Secure workflow orchestrator connecting the frontend request to enterprise AI services without exposing API credentials.
  • Google Gemini Model: Advanced multimodal reasoning engine designed to interpret clinical queries, medical terminology, and contextual health discussions.
Safety First

Core Principles

  • Empathetic & Calm: Healthcare inquiries can be stressful. We prioritize a reassuring, helpful tone.
  • Transparent Limitations: Never pretend to possess human clinical judgment or medical licenses.
  • Human-in-the-Loop: The AI serves as an informational bridge, not a substitute for a licensed physician.
  • No Commercial Influences: No sponsored pharmaceutical promotions or biased medical recommendations.

Privacy & Local Storage

Your privacy and health confidentiality are fundamental priorities. Here is how your data is handled:

  • No Mandatory Personal Information: MediMind AI does not ask for or require your name, address, telephone number, national ID, or insurance details.
  • Local Browser Storage: To let you review previous discussions, conversation titles and text messages are stored strictly inside your browser's local storage (localStorage). This data stays on your device.
  • Media Files Not Persisted: Uploaded images or previewed videos are processed in memory and are NOT saved permanently into your browser's local storage.
  • Backend Communication: When an n8n webhook URL is configured, messages are transmitted directly over HTTPS to your designated workflow. No third-party ad trackers or analytics scripts are embedded.
  • Instant Data Purge: You can completely erase all stored chats from your browser at any time using the "Clear Local History" button in the sidebar.
